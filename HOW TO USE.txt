ENGLISH WITH JAMES COURSE
=========================
324 video lessons from the "ENGLISH with James · engVid" YouTube channel,
organized into 8 units and 38 chapters, with a 3-question quiz for every lesson.

HOW TO OPEN IT
--------------
On this computer: double-click "Start Course.bat". It opens the course in your
web browser at http://localhost:8765. Keep the black window open while studying
and close it when you're done.

(YouTube doesn't let videos play when index.html is opened by double-clicking,
so use Start Course.bat. If a video still won't play, every lesson has a
"Watch it on YouTube" link under the video.)

ON ANOTHER DEVICE (phone, tablet, her own laptop)
-------------------------------------------------
Put the course online for free, then open the link on any device:
  1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
  2. Drag this whole "english-with-james-course" folder onto the page.
  3. Open the link it gives you (you can bookmark it).
GitHub Pages works too.

HOW PROGRESS WORKS
------------------
- Watch the video, answer the 3 quiz questions, and press "Check answers".
- Getting 2 or 3 right marks the lesson complete (green check). Retry any time.
- Progress is saved in the browser she uses, so she should stick to the same
  browser and device. "Reset progress" is at the bottom of every page.
- "Hide grown-up topics" (bottom of the page) hides 8 lessons about drinking,
  rude slang, pregnancy, and dating/gender words.

CHANGING THE COURSE
-------------------
data/outline.txt     units, chapters, and the order of lessons
data/quiz1-5.txt     the quiz questions (question | right answer | wrong | wrong)
Rebuild after editing:
  node tools/build.js data/videos.json data course-data.js
