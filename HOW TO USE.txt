ENGLISH WITH JAMES COURSE
=========================
316 video lessons from the "ENGLISH with James · engVid" YouTube channel,
organized into 8 units and 38 chapters, with a 3-question quiz for every lesson
and a 10-question test for every unit.

HOW TO OPEN IT
--------------
Online (any device): https://py-piorg.github.io/english-with-james-course/

On this computer without internet hosting: double-click "Start Course.bat". It
opens the course at http://localhost:8765. Keep the black window open while
studying. (YouTube doesn't let videos play when index.html is opened by
double-clicking. Every lesson also has a "Watch it on YouTube" link.)

HOW PROGRESS WORKS
------------------
- Lessons: watch the video, answer the 3 quiz questions, press "Check answers".
  2 or 3 right marks the lesson complete (green check). Retry any time.
- Wrong answers show the right answer underneath.
- Pop quizzes: after she presses Play, the lesson's 3 quiz questions pause the
  video at the moment that topic is taught. Her answers there are filled into
  the quiz below the video, so at the end she just presses "Check answers".
  Dots under the video show which questions she got right. Use the
  "Full screen" button under the video (not YouTube's) so questions still show.
- Unit tests: at the bottom of each unit page. 10 questions mixed from that
  unit's lessons, a new mix every try. 8 right passes. Missed questions link
  back to the lesson they came from.
- The home page shows overall progress, unit tests passed, and how many days
  in a row she has studied.
- Progress is saved in the browser she uses. To move it to another device, use
  "Move or back up progress" at the bottom of any page: copy the code on one
  device and paste it under "Restore" on the other.
- "Reset progress" is at the bottom of every page.
- The round button at the top right switches between auto, light, and dark.
- Press / on a keyboard to jump to search; Enter opens the first result.

LEFT OUT ON PURPOSE
-------------------
8 channel videos are not in the course (drinking, rude slang, pregnancy, and
sex/gender vocabulary). They are listed on the "X" line in data/outline.txt.

CHANGING THE COURSE
-------------------
data/outline.txt     units, chapters, lesson order, and left-out videos
data/quiz1-5.txt     the quiz questions (question | right answer | wrong | wrong)
data/checkpoints.json when each question pops up (seconds into the video)
Rebuild after editing, then commit and push to update the website:
  node tools/build.js data/videos.json data course-data.js
